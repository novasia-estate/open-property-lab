Drag a box on the source. Each box has equal importance. For keyboard entry, use the pixel-coordinate fields below. Maximum 12 regions.

    2. Compare formats

    Crop windows use the largest possible source area at each aspect ratio. Among a finite set of edge/centre placements, the proposal first maximises the worst retained region, then average retention. This is a disclosed candidate search, not a guarantee of a global optimum or aesthetic quality.

    3. Save prepared exports

    Click a file link below to save it. Your browser may ask where to download. JSON is also shown for copying.

    Method, privacy and limits

    No upload, analytics, remote fonts or dependencies are used by this tool. The host may collect its own ordinary site analytics. Local inputs are limited to JPEG/PNG/WebP, 8 MiB and 16 million decoded pixels. Files are decoded by your browser; reject errors are shown. The dimension limit is checked after decoding, so do not open untrusted decompression bombs.

    Retention is the intersected rectangle area divided by the annotated rectangle area. It describes your boxes, not automatic subject recognition. Crop coordinates are in decoded source pixels and can be fractional. PNG exports are capped at 1600 pixels on the longest side and contain the rendered crop without review outlines; source metadata is not deliberately copied. This does not certify anonymisation. The JSON records the original file SHA-256 when available and omits its filename. Recheck annotations whenever the source changes.

    Works offline once downloaded. Pointer and numeric region entry are supported. No annotations are stored after closing or reloading.
